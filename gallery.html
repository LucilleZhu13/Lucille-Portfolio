Wishes from Me to You

Introduction

Our game “Wishes from Me to You” is a pixel-style small adventure game with the game setting: You are a messenger of Santa Claus in the Santa Village. Santa Claus has lost three pieces of precious gifts. You are now responsible for gathering the gifts before Christmas to make sure this year’s Christmas is perfect!

The player will be immersed in a winter village, complete certain tasks, and reveal the surprise that awaits them at the end of the game. Our goal for this game is to bring a surprising gaming experience for the players to welcome them to a happy Christmas season!

Try the game here!

Intro Page

The goal of this game is getting through all maps within the time limit. The play can control their avatar using arrow keys or WASD. The intro page also servers as a tutorial that the player needs to pass this map to move on.

To pass the maps, the played may need to collect gifts on the way, go through a portal, or get the key to go through the door, which is also hinted in this tutorial level.

One Button UI

Introduction

A screen-based interface built around a single button with an unusual or artistic function. The design explores how one button can communicate its purpose through appearance and behavior, including how it's pressed, what happens when it's pressed once or multiple times, and how the interface shows feedback or progress. The button’s design balances ease of use with the risk of accidental interaction, creating a focused and intentional user experience.

Version 1.0

This is a one button self-track app that the user can record their feelings and emotions through sliding the button on the scale. This UI design aimed for a minimal interface that is easy to understand and navigate.

This version further simplified the interaction by removing unnecessary features like the chart and calendar from the main page.

JargonAI

User Scenario

You are a young adult starting a corporate career after recently graduating from college. You have a large family, with relatives around the globe.

Sometimes, it’s hard to connect with your family because you can’t always understand their language or certain aspects of their culture. It’s also difficult for them to understand you, as some of them are much older than you and not familiar with your Gen Z slang.

Also, you find yourself feeling lost at work a lot after sitting in meetings with those in other departments or those with many years of experience. There is a lot you need to learn about the company, like what certain acronyms mean and how different departments interact.

Lastly, outside of work, you are trying to pick up a new niche hobby. Unfortunately, there are only a few online creators that post information and it’s difficult to digest as a beginner.

In all of these scenarios, you could use JargonAI to provide extra help and understanding with minimal effort on your behalf.

One Button UI

Introduction

In a future world where gender exists as a spectrum of numbers rather than fixed categories, social norms and stereotypes tied to binary gender roles have been abolished. People are born sexless through painless surgery and can later choose their sex or remain neutral. Families are redefined without “mother” or “father,” and parenting requires government approval. This society embraces equality and freedom of expression, though pressures to conform to “genderless” ideals still create new forms of judgment. The project’s artifact, StylesUp, is a Figma-based outfit-generating app that helps users design looks based on their position on the gender spectrum, style preferences, and occasions. Featuring gradient-based aesthetics symbolizing fluidity, it connects users through AI-generated outfits and SmartMirror try-ons, evolving into a major social platform that fosters creativity and inclusivity while highlighting how even a seemingly equal world can develop its own biases.
